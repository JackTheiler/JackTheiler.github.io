Omaha Flood Risk and Diversity Map

This map combines flood-risk data, floodplain zones, income, population, and diversity data to show where flood concern may overlap with potentially vulnerable communities.

How to read this map
Red areas show higher flood concern, yellow areas show medium flood concern, and green areas show lower flood concern. Blue areas show floodplain zones. Orange tracts show diversity patterns. Click a tract or floodplain area for more details.

Data note: Floodplain data layers were prepared and processed with the assistance of AI tools.
